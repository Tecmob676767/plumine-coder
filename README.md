# Plumine Coder - Web Creator Studio & Client Request Portal

Welcome to **Plumine Coder** — an online web creator platform crafted for web developers to showcase their services and receive custom website orders from clients at a transparent, fixed price of **₹500 only per single website**.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & How It Works

### 1. Fixed Pricing: ₹500 Only
- Prominently displays the flat **₹500 / single website** guarantee across the hero, cards, badges, and request summaries.
- Includes responsive layout, mobile/desktop support, WhatsApp order/chat buttons, contact forms, and 24–48 hour rapid turnaround.

### 2. Client Login with Name & Phone Number
- No cumbersome email confirmations or forgotten passwords.
- Clients simply enter their **Full Name** and **10-Digit Mobile Number**.
- The portal remembers their session and ties all submitted requests to their phone number.

### 3. How You (The Creator) Receive & Manage Requests
The platform provides 3 distinct ways to receive and manage all client requests:

1. **Direct 1-Click WhatsApp Dispatch**:
   - As soon as a client submits their website requirements, a formatted project summary is generated.
   - The client can click **"Send Directly to Plumine Coder on WhatsApp"**, opening WhatsApp with all details (Order ID, Name, Phone, Features, Notes) pre-typed to send straight to your WhatsApp number.

2. **Built-in Creator Admin Hub**:
   - Click the **"Creator Admin"** button in the top navigation bar (or footer).
   - View real-time statistics: *Total Requests, New/Pending, In Progress, and Completed*.
   - Filter and search incoming orders by client name, phone number, or Order ID.
   - 1-Click **"Chat Client"** or **"Call Client"** buttons for immediate contact.
   - Dropdown status changer (`New Request` ➔ `In Progress` ➔ `Ready for Review` ➔ `Completed`).
   - **Export CSV Button**: Instantly download all submitted requests into an Excel/CSV spreadsheet.

3. **Creator WhatsApp Settings**:
   - In the Creator Admin Hub, click **"WhatsApp Settings"** to set your personal phone number (with country code, e.g. `919876543210`).
   - All WhatsApp dispatch buttons will immediately route to your configured number.
